FAQ

Got Questions? We've Got Answers.

How long does a typical website project take? ⌄
Most projects are completed within 2–4 weeks depending on complexity. Our Starter package typically goes live in 10–14 days. We always agree on a timeline upfront before work begins.
Do you work with businesses outside India? ⌄
Absolutely. We work with clients globally. All communication is in English and we're comfortable across different time zones. Payments can be made in INR or USD.
What platforms do you build on? ⌄
We primarily build with Next.js, React, and WordPress. For e-commerce we use Shopify and WooCommerce. We choose the right tool for your specific goals, not just what we're most comfortable with.
Will I be able to update the site myself after launch? ⌄
Yes. We build with CMS options (Sanity, WordPress, or Contentful) so you can update text, images, and content without touching code. We also provide a walkthrough video after launch.
What's included in your maintenance plans? ⌄
Our maintenance plans cover plugin/dependency updates, uptime monitoring, security patches, monthly backups, and a set number of content change requests. We offer monthly and annual plans.
Do you offer payment plans? ⌄
Yes. We typically work on a 50% deposit + 50% on delivery model. For larger projects, we can split into 3 milestone payments. We're flexible and happy to discuss what works for you.
